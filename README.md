# DL Connect

Create a modern, premium, responsive business website for **DL Talent & Technology**.

### Company Information

**Company Name:** DL Talent & Technology

**Tagline:** Connecting Talent. Delivering Technology. Driving Growth.

**Company Description:**
DL Talent & Technology provides Recruitment, HR Support, Virtual Assistance, Administrative Support, Executive Administrative Assistance, Customer Support, Data Entry, Email Marketing, Technical Writing, Content Writing, and Web Development services. We help startups, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses streamline operations, improve productivity, and access skilled talent and technology solutions through cost-effective and scalable support services.

---

## Design Requirements

* Modern corporate design
* Premium Blue, White, and Black color palette
* Professional UI/UX
* Fully responsive (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile)
* Smooth animations and scroll effects
* SEO-friendly structure
* Fast-loading performance
* Modern illustrations and professional icons
* Clean typography
* Strong call-to-action sections
* Professional business branding
